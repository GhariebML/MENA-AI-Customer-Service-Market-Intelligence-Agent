# Quick Research Objectives & Strategic Framework

**Project:** MENA AI Customer Service Automation Market Intelligence  
**Student:** Mohamed Abd Elsalam Mohamed Gharieb  
**Course:** AWS AI & ML Scholars Program — Course 3: Building No-Code AI Agents with Amazon Quick  
**Strategic Question:**  
> *"How attractive is the market opportunity for AI-powered customer service automation platforms serving SMEs in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE, and what competitive positioning, product differentiation, and market-entry strategy should a new solution (e.g., NexaServe) consider?"*

---

## Four Core Quick Research Streams

| Stream ID | Research Task | Strategic Objective | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QR-01** | **Market Overview — Egypt, Saudi Arabia & UAE** | Identify country-specific AI strategies, SME base counts, digital infrastructure signals, and customer-facing AI adoption evidence. | Separates top-down policy enthusiasm from real SME purchasing power and avoids treating MENA as a single uniform market. |
| **QR-02** | **Competitive Intelligence & Pricing Models** | Benchmark global SaaS platforms (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom Fin, Salesforce Agentforce) and verifiable MENA regional/Arabic-first players (Unifonic, CEQUENS, ZIWO AI). | Reveals commercial meter fragmentation (seat, session, outcome, conversation, credit), Arabic support realities, and regional differentiation gaps. |
| **QR-03** | **SME Customer Needs & Automation Use Cases** | Investigate SME support pain points, WhatsApp Business usage, Meta's July 2025 per-message pricing shift, dialect/voice-note friction, and human handoff requirements. | Ensures product design solves real operational bottlenecks rather than building generic FAQ chatbots. |
| **QR-04** | **Technology, Privacy & Regulation** | Map binding personal data protection laws (Egypt Law 151/2020 & Decree 816/2025; KSA PDPL & SDAIA transfer rules; UAE Decree-Law 45/2021) and Agentic/RAG deployment architecture. | Treats compliance, cross-border data transfers, and tenant isolation as Day-1 product architecture gates rather than late-stage legal checklists. |
