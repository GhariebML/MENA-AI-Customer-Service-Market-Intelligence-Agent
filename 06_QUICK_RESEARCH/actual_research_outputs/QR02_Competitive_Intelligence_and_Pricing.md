**Research Task:** QR-02 — Competitive Landscape & Pricing

**Scope:** 4 Global SaaS Leaders + 3 Verifiable MENA Regional / Arabic-First Platforms

---

## 1. Core Finding: Commercial Meters Are Fragmented; Arabic Claims Outpace Dialect Proof

Global support platforms charge across four incompatible billing units—**per agent seat**, **seat + AI session pack**, **per resolved outcome**, and **per conversation / Flex Credits**—making headline list-price rankings deceptive. Meanwhile, regional MENA platforms (**Unifonic, CEQUENS, ZIWO AI**) compete directly on native WhatsApp Business API integration, local data hosting, and regional Arabic/voice workflows.

## 4. Competitive Differentiation Gap for a New Entrant (e.g., NexaServe)

1. **Avoid Competing on "Arabic Checkbox":** Zendesk, Intercom, Unifonic, CEQUENS, and ZIWO already claim Arabic support. Differentiation requires **verified dialect accuracy (Egyptian + Saudi + code-switching)** and task completion, not a language flag.
2. **Predictable Hybrid Pricing:** SMEs fear unpredictable overage spikes (`$49/100` sessions on Freshdesk or `$2/conv` on Agentforce) combined with Meta's July 2025 per-template WhatsApp billing. A transparent monthly allowance + capped overage / verified-resolution model should be tested.
3. **Middle-Ground Niche:** Global suites are USD-priced and generic on MENA workflows (e.g., COD verification, WhatsApp voice notes, Fawry/Mada/Tamara payment triage); regional CPaaS leaders like Unifonic start at enterprise tiers (`$499+/mo`). An SME-accessible, WhatsApp-native bilingual RAG + ERP/OMS action layer sits between these tiers—if unit economics work.
