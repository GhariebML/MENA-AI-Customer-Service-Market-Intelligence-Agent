## QR-01 — Regional Market & Policy Signals (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE)

> Conduct a source-backed market intelligence scan, current to November 2026, of AI-powered customer-service automation for SMEs in **Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates**. Research each country separately. Identify official AI strategies (Egypt National AI Strategy 2025–2030, Saudi CST/SDAIA guidance, UAE National AI Strategy 2031 / TDRA), SME digitalization indicators, cloud/SaaS adoption, and documented customer-facing AI deployments. Prioritize government, regulator, and statistical sources. Clearly separate policy intent from measured commercial SME purchasing demand. Do not fabricate TAM/SAM/SOM estimates. Return 5–8 dated signals per market with exact title, publisher, URL, publication date, claim, and confidence note.

## QR-02 — Competitive Intelligence & Commercial Metering

> Compare **Zendesk AI Agents, Freshdesk Freddy AI, Intercom Fin, Salesforce Agentforce**, and verifiable regional MENA/Arabic-first providers (**Unifonic, CEQUENS, ZIWO AI**). For each vendor, document: target customer segment, product positioning, Arabic language support (distinguishing Modern Standard Arabic from Egyptian/Saudi/Gulf dialect performance), WhatsApp Business API and CRM integrations, human handoff workflows, analytics, deployment options, and publicly listed pricing (currency, billing unit, included usage, overage rules, and access date). Prefer official product, pricing, and help pages. Do not compare unlike billing meters as equivalent without normalizing workload volume.

## QR-03 — SME Customer Needs, WhatsApp Channels & Use Cases

> Research public evidence regarding SME customer-service pain points and preferred communication channels in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE across e-commerce/retail, logistics, and digital services. Focus on WhatsApp Business adoption, Meta's per-message template pricing update (effective July 2025), response-time expectations, staffing constraints, bilingual/dialect support, and human-in-the-loop escalation. Explicitly distinguish verified industry surveys or case studies from analyst hypotheses, and specify discovery interview questions required to validate local willingness-to-pay.

## QR-04 — Privacy, Data Residency, Governance & Technology Architecture

> Build a country-by-country regulatory and technical architecture signal map for Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE covering an AI-powered SaaS customer-service platform using RAG and WhatsApp/CRM integrations. Examine controller vs. processor obligations, lawful basis/consent, cross-border data transfers, breach reporting, retention, automated decision oversight, and cloud vs. private hosting trade-offs under Egypt's Law 151/2020 (and Decree 816/2025), Saudi Arabia's PDPL (SDAIA regulations), and the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021. Use official regulator and government publications first. Do not offer formal legal advice; list concrete questions for local counsel.
